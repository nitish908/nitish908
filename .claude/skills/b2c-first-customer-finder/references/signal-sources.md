# B2C Signal Sources by Category

Starting map of where consumers publicly vent, ask, or complain. Always confirm a subreddit/group
still exists and is active before relying on it — communities die, merge, or go private.

## Local services (home repair, fitness, salons, pet care, tutoring, cleaning, etc.)
- `r/[CityName]` and `r/AskaCityName` subreddits — "does anyone know a good ___" is a recurring
  thread format
- Nextdoor (not directly searchable by Claude's web tools, but Google-indexed posts sometimes
  surface — verify before citing)
- Google Maps / Yelp reviews of the closest competitors — 1–2 star reviews are direct pain signals
  with a name and a specific complaint
- Local Facebook Groups ("[Neighborhood] Buy Nothing / Recommendations") — often not indexed by
  search; if the user has access, they can search these manually using this skill's ICP framing
- `r/HomeImprovement`, `r/personalfinance` (for cost complaints), category-specific subs
  (`r/Fitness`, `r/skincareaddiction`, `r/dogtraining`, etc.)

## Consumer apps / subscription products
- App Store and Google Play reviews of direct competitors — 1–3 star reviews often state the exact
  missing feature or dealbreaker
- Reddit threads titled "alternatives to [competitor]" or "[competitor] sucks now"
- Product Hunt comment sections on adjacent/competing launches
- Twitter/X search for `"[competitor]" "switching from"` or `"anyone else hate"`

## Physical products (DTC goods)
- Amazon review complaints on the closest substitute product (sort by 1-star, filter recent)
- Reddit `r/BuyItForLife`, category subs (`r/EDC`, `r/BabyBumps` for baby products, etc.)
- TikTok/Instagram comment sections under competitor ads or viral posts about the problem category

## Health, wellness, personal finance, life-stage products
- Life-stage subreddits: `r/newparents`, `r/personalfinance`, `r/loseit`, `r/QuittingSmoking`, etc.
- These communities are especially sensitive — bias toward observation/data-gathering over direct
  outreach unless the person is explicitly asking for recommendations

## General cross-category signals
- `site:reddit.com "does anyone know" [problem]`
- `site:reddit.com "recommend" [category] -sponsored`
- `"PSA" [competitor or problem]` — PSA-style posts are often unprompted pain signals
- Review aggregator sites (Trustpilot, G2 for consumer software, BBB complaints) for named
  competitors

## What NOT to treat as a signal source
- Anything requiring login/scraping to view (private groups, gated forums)
- Marketplaces of personal data (people-search sites, leaked databases, scraped contact lists)
- Anyone under 18 discussing personal problems — exclude entirely regardless of relevance
- Posts where the person has since deleted the account/post (respect the deletion, don't cite
  cached/archived versions to route around it)
